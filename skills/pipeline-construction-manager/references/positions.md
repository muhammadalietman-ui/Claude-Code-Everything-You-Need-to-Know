# Site positions, skills, communication and meetings (DE035)

Use in Staffing mode, and whenever a case needs to know who does what. Experience minimums and headcounts are **proposals for hiring**, not contract figures. Check them against any staffing requirements in the contract (key personnel, qualifications) before use. Certifications marked 'confirm' must be checked against the DE035 specification.

## Positions

### P01 Construction Manager (Bauleiter / Oberbauleiter) — CM Office
- **Reports to:** Project Manager (JV: SICIM Deputy PM / DENYS PM) | **Headcount:** 1 | **Hire by:** Pre-mobilisation
- **Function:** Leads all site departments; owns the Obligation Register, Gap Register and Ready-to-Start gates; single site voice to the OGE site representative.
- **Responsibilities:** Run the department organisation (D01-D14); chair daily coordination and weekly gap cycle; approve GO / NO-GO for every front, crossing, tie-in and test; decide trade-offs between schedule, cost and quality; ensure every client instruction is logged and assessed for notice; escalate to PM / PD / JV per DoA.
- **Technical skills:** Large-diameter onshore pipeline construction end to end (ROW to reinstatement); welding/NDT/coating acceptance logic; HDD and crossing methods; hydrotest and tie-ins; spread productivity and recovery planning.
- **Contract & documentation skills:** Reads and applies contract conditions, SOW, specifications and permits; notices and time bars; instruction vs variation; German construction law concepts (VOB/B, BGB: Behinderungsanzeige, Bedenkenanzeige, Nachtrag, Abnahme) with Contracts/Legal confirmation; back-to-back subcontracting.
- **Soft skills:** Decisive leadership under pressure; conflict resolution between departments and JV partners; clear escalation; coaching; client relationship management.
- **Qualifications / experience (proposed):** Engineering degree (civil, mechanical, petroleum or equivalent); proposed minimum 15 years in pipeline construction, of which 5 as CM/spread manager on DN 800+ lines.
- **Certifications / licences:** SCC for operational managers (SCC-Dok) or equivalent; knowledge of BaustellV / SiGeKo coordination; DVGW pipeline practice (GW 301 company scope).
- **Languages:** German and English business level (JV and OGE); Italian or French an advantage (SICIM / DENYS).
- **Documents owned / must read:** Contract agreement and order of precedence; particular conditions; SOW; master schedule; permits summary; JV DoA.
- **Meetings:** M01, M02, M03, M05, M06, M07, M08, M10, M11, M12, M13, M14
- **KPIs:** Milestones met; zero LTI; open gaps past need date; notices on time; cost to complete vs budget.

### P03 Spread Superintendent (Spread-Leiter / Bauabschnittsleiter) — D01 Site Operations
- **Reports to:** Construction Manager | **Headcount:** 1 per spread | **Hire by:** Mobilisation
- **Function:** Delivers daily production across the spread safely, to spec and in balance.
- **Responsibilities:** Plan daily fronts with foremen; balance welding, coating, trenching, lowering-in and backfill; daily report and delay/disruption record; enforce PTW and ITP holds; first point for subcontractor site instructions.
- **Technical skills:** Spread sequence and crew/equipment balance; welding productivity drivers; trench stability and open-trench limits; lowering-in and side-boom lifting; reinstatement and topsoil handling.
- **Contract & documentation skills:** Reads method statements, ITPs, permit conditions and landowner restrictions for each front; records instructions and disruptions daily.
- **Soft skills:** Hands-on leadership of large crews; fast problem solving; safety ownership.
- **Qualifications / experience (proposed):** Proposed minimum 12 years pipeline construction, 5 as superintendent on large-diameter lines.
- **Certifications / licences:** SCC-Dok; first aider; RSA 21 / MVAS traffic safety awareness.
- **Languages:** German or English with working command of the other; crew languages an advantage.
- **Documents owned / must read:** Method statements; ITPs; alignment sheets; permit conditions per section; 3-week look-ahead.
- **Meetings:** M01, M02, M03, M04, M05, M06, M09, M12
- **KPIs:** Planned vs actual metres per activity; spread balance; idle hours with cause.

## Communication protocol

1. Only the Construction Manager, Project Manager or persons they name in writing receive instructions from the OGE site representative. Anyone else receiving a client instruction records it and passes it to the Spread Superintendent the same day; it goes into the instruction log and is screened by Contracts for a notice.
2. Nobody agrees scope, price or programme changes with the client, a subcontractor or a landowner outside the contract procedure. Commitments are made in writing and logged.
3. Letters to OGE, authorities and subcontractors are drafted by the owning department, checked by Contracts and signed per the JV DoA. They are bilingual German/English where they go to German parties.
4. Department-to-department requests go in writing (email or form) with an owner and a need date. If a request has no owner, it goes to the interface review (M05).
5. Escalation ladder: department lead, then CM (same day), then Project Manager (within 24 h for critical-path, cost or entitlement issues), then Project Director / JV (per DoA). Safety and stop-work issues escalate immediately.
6. Meeting minutes are issued within the time set in the meeting procedure, list actions with owner and due date, and are filed by Document Control as contemporaneous records.
7. Shared DE035/DE042 resources (Emstek yard, plant, procurement): every request and every cost states the project code.
