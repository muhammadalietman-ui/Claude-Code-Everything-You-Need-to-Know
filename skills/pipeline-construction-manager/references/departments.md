# Department charters under the Construction Manager

Use the charter of each department a case touches. Answer *as* that specialist: what they read, what they check, what gap they see, what they deliver, and when they escalate. KPI targets are never invented — take them from the contract, specification, project plan or the user; otherwise write "target per contract/spec — to confirm".

Every charter has the same structure:
- **Mission** — one line.
- **Extract from the contract** — what this department must pull out of the client documents into the Obligation Register.
- **Standing outputs** — what it produces and keeps current.
- **Gap triggers** — situations that must create a Gap Register entry (type G1–G10).
- **KPIs** — what the CM measures it on.
- **Hand-offs** — receives from / gives to (each one is a potential G2 gap).
- **Escalate to CM when** — the threshold for the CM's decision.

## D01 Site Operations / Spread
**Mission:** Turn the plan into metres of pipe in the ground, safely and to spec, every day.
**Extract from the contract:** working width and ROW limits; working hours and day/night restrictions; open-trench length limits; access roads and entry points; seasonal or ecological windows; traffic and public interface conditions; landowner restrictions per parcel; client attendance requirements at site.
**Standing outputs:** daily report (activity, quantities, crew, plant, weather, delays, instructions received); 3-week look-ahead by front (with D03); front release status by section; daily toolbox talk record (with D08); delay and disruption record sheet (daily, signed by the client rep where possible — feeds D04).
**Gap triggers:** front not released but crew mobilised (G9); activity started without approved MS/ITP (G9/G5); client rep instruction given verbally (G10 → D04 within 24 h); productivity below planned basis for consecutive days (G7); spread imbalance (welding outrunning coating or lowering-in) (G7); open trench above limit (G6/HSE).
**KPIs:** planned vs actual quantity per activity; spread balance between pacemaker and following crews; idle crew/plant hours with cause; first-time-right rate (with D07).
**Hand-offs:** receives released fronts (D09), approved drawings (D02), material at stringing point (D06), plant (D10), PTW (D08); gives daily quantities to D03/D12, records to D13, instructions log to D04.
**Escalate to CM when:** any stop-work; a front is blocked longer than the look-ahead float; a client instruction changes method, sequence or scope; a subcontractor refuses an instruction.

## D02 Field Engineering & Survey
**Mission:** Make the design buildable and prove what was built.
**Extract from the contract:** drawing list and approval status; alignment sheets; crossing details and owner conditions (road, rail, water, foreign utilities); cover depths; bending and pipe-allocation rules; tolerance and survey requirements; temporary-works approval rules; as-built format and coordinate system; TQ and RFI procedure and response times.
**Standing outputs:** construction drawing register (IFC status, revision); crossing and tie-in schedule; TQ/RFI log with response due dates; pipe book / bend allocation; as-built survey per section; temporary works register; quantity take-off for BOQ and measurement (with D12).
**Gap triggers:** drawing not IFC at need date (G5); TQ response overdue against activity start (G5/G9); site condition differs from drawings (utility not shown, ground) (G1 → D04 for notice); as-built backlog behind construction (G10); temporary work without design/approval (G9/HSE).
**KPIs:** TQs open vs overdue; drawings IFC vs needed; as-built lag (metres built minus metres surveyed).
**Hand-offs:** gives drawings to D01, quantities to D12, crossing data to D09, test sections to D11, as-builts to D13; receives conditions from D09, specs from D07.
**Escalate to CM when:** a design change affects scope, cost or schedule; a differing site condition is found (notice clock may start).

## D03 Planning & Project Controls
**Mission:** Keep a true schedule, see the critical path move before it moves, and keep the delay record that wins or loses EOT.
**Extract from the contract:** key dates, milestones and sectional completion; programme submission and update obligations (format, frequency); delay damages basis (as given); EOT procedure; progress measurement rules; payment milestones tied to progress; restricted windows (permits, shutdowns, seasons).
**Standing outputs:** baseline and current schedule; 3-week look-ahead; weekly progress (earned vs planned); critical path and float report; delay events register (event, date, cause, affected activities, notice ref — with D04); gap-need-date list (every open gap linked to the activity it blocks); recovery levers list.
**Gap triggers:** an open gap whose closure date is later than its need date (G9 — highest planning priority); float consumption on near-critical path; long-lead item ROS later than need (G8); client approval durations longer than allowed in the schedule (G5); schedule update not submitted per contract (G5).
**KPIs:** SPI or planned vs actual progress; critical float trend; number of gaps threatening need dates; delay events with notice issued vs without.
**Hand-offs:** receives quantities from D01, delivery dates from D05/D06, approval status from D13, permit windows from D09; gives look-ahead to all, delay register to D04, progress to D12.
**Escalate to CM when:** a milestone or payment milestone moves; critical path changes; a delay event without a notice is identified.

## D04 Contracts & Claims
**Mission:** Protect entitlement, keep notices on time and make every subcontract back-to-back.
**Extract from the contract:** order of precedence; notice provisions and time limits (as given — never invent); variation/instruction procedure; EOT and cost entitlement events; payment, retention and guarantee terms; liability caps, damages and indemnities; suspension and stop-work rights; dispute steps; governing law (German law — BGB/VOB/B where applicable, to be confirmed); insurance obligations.
**Standing outputs:** notices calendar (event, trigger date, time limit, due date, sent, acknowledgement); instruction & variation register (instructed, priced, submitted, agreed, paid); claim files (event, entitlement basis, records, quantum with D12); back-to-back matrix (main-contract obligation ↔ subcontract clause); correspondence log.
**Gap triggers:** any instruction or event without notice assessment within the internal response time (G3 — top priority); work proceeding on a disputed instruction without reservation (G3); main-contract obligation not flowed down (G4); subcontract term weaker than main contract (G4); unclear JV signatory for a letter (G5).
**KPIs:** notices issued on time; variations submitted vs agreed vs paid; claim value under substantiation vs records available; back-to-back coverage of active packages.
**Hand-offs:** receives instructions log (D01), delay register (D03), cost data (D12), NCRs/quality disputes (D07), permit changes (D09); gives contract interpretation to all, flow-down terms to D05.
**Escalate to CM when:** a time bar is within the internal warning period; entitlement is disputed by OGE; a letter needs JV/PD signature.
**Rule:** every legal conclusion is marked "to be verified by Contracts/Legal"; use dr-sherif-el-haggan for FIDIC/claims reasoning and subcontract-gap-check for flow-down review.

## D05 Subcontracts & Procurement
**Mission:** The right package, supplier and PO in place before the need date, compliant with the DoA and back-to-back with the client contract.
**Extract from the contract:** approved vendor / subcontractor approval requirements; client approval of subcontractors; mandatory specifications and certifications for materials and services; local content or labour conditions (if any, as given); flow-down clauses (HSE, quality, insurance, confidentiality, audit).
**Standing outputs:** package plan (package, scope, strategy, need date, status); MRF → RFQ → CBE → PO tracker on the weekly SOP rhythm (MRF Mon–Tue, CBE Wed, PO Thu, invoice verification Fri); supplier status register (approved, blocked, prepayment, insurance/certificates valid); expediting report; subcontract scope-interface register (with D04).
**Gap triggers:** work started before PO (G3); scope item not in any package (G1); package interface unpriced on both sides (G2 — e.g. S-02 Lorenz Bohrtechnik); supplier blocked or refusing terms before need date (G8); PO value beyond DoA without approval (G5); certificates/insurance expired (G10).
**KPIs:** POs issued before need date; retroactive POs (count and value); CBE cycle time; open MRFs past need date; supplier account issues open.
**Hand-offs:** receives MRFs from all departments, flow-down terms from D04, specs from D07, need dates from D03; gives POs and delivery dates to D06/D03, contracts to D01/D12.
**Escalate to CM when:** a critical-path package has no viable supplier; a retroactive PO is requested; DoA approval needed from Alexander Matheis or JV.
**Use:** subcontract-drafter, subcontract-addendum, subcontract-gap-check, subcontract-cbe, material-service-cbe.

## D06 Materials, Logistics & Pipe Yard
**Mission:** Traceable, undamaged material at the right stringing point, and a clean record of what the client supplied and in what condition.
**Extract from the contract:** client/free-issue material list (line pipe, bends, valves?) and transfer-of-custody rules; receipt inspection and damage-reporting time limits; storage and preservation requirements; traceability (heat number, pipe number) requirements; surplus and scrap return rules.
**Standing outputs:** material receipt register (with inspection result and damage report); free-issue reconciliation (received, issued, installed, surplus, scrap); pipe tally and traceability chain (pipe no. → weld no., with D07); yard storage and preservation log; stringing logistics plan with transport permits (StVO/StVZO where applicable).
**Gap triggers:** damage at receipt not reported within the contract window (G10/G3 — liability shifts); free-issue shortfall vs need (G8); traceability break (G10 — integrity); material for DE042 and DE035 mixed in the shared Emstek yard without allocation (G2); preservation lapse (coating UV, end caps) (quality).
**KPIs:** receipt inspections within time limit; free-issue reconciliation variance; traceability completeness; material-caused idle time.
**Hand-offs:** receives deliveries from D05, allocation from D02; gives traceability to D07/D13, consumption to D12.
**Escalate to CM when:** free-issue material is damaged or short (notice to OGE via D04); a traceability gap affects already-welded pipe.

## D07 QA/QC, Welding & NDT
**Mission:** Pipeline integrity proven by records — every weld, joint and test accepted against the governing spec.
**Extract from the contract:** quality plan requirements; governing specifications and standards with editions (API 1104 / EN 12732 / DVGW / DIN 30670 etc. — confirm against DE035 spec); ITP hold/witness points and notice periods for client attendance; WPS/PQR and welder qualification approval rules; NDT method, extent and acceptance criteria; coating system and holiday test criteria; NCR and concession procedure; calibration requirements; dossier quality content.
**Standing outputs:** ITP register per activity with signed points; WPS/PQR and welder qualification register; weld map and NDT result log (repair rate by welder/crew/WPS); coating and holiday-test log; NCR register (open, root cause, disposition, closure); calibration register; quality records index for D13.
**Gap triggers:** activity at a hold point without client release (G9); welder working without valid qualification (G10 — integrity); repair rate trend above spec/plan basis (quality → D01 root cause); NCR open past disposition date (G10); coating defects attributable to material or applicator (G3 — e.g. S-01 DENSO/DENSOLEN); NDT backlog behind welding (G7).
**KPIs:** weld repair rate; NDT backlog (welds); coating holiday rate; NCRs open/overdue; ITP points signed on time; records completeness per km.
**Hand-offs:** receives traceability from D06, NDT subcontract from D05, methods from D01; gives acceptance to D11 (test readiness), records to D13, quality disputes to D04.
**Escalate to CM when:** a systemic defect is found; client rejects work; a concession is needed; quality conflicts with recovery plan (CM decides, integrity has priority).

## D08 HSE
**Mission:** Nobody hurt, nothing spilled, and every task under a valid permit and risk assessment.
**Extract from the contract:** client HSE requirements and OGE house style; reporting obligations and time limits for incidents; mandatory inductions, PPE, competence cards; permit-to-work rules (excavation, lifting, hot work, confined space, hydrotest zones); traffic management obligations (StVO); German regulatory references (DGUV, DIN, WHG, AwSV as applicable); HSE penalties or stop-work provisions.
**Standing outputs:** HSE plan; task risk assessments linked to method statements; PTW register; induction and competence register (including subcontractors); incident and near-miss register with investigation status; inspection/audit schedule and findings; emergency response plan per front.
**Gap triggers:** task without risk assessment or PTW (G9 — stop); subcontractor workers without induction/competence evidence (G10); incident report not within client time limit (G10/G3); hydrotest exclusion zone not defined (G9); HSE requirement not flowed down (G4 → D04/D05).
**KPIs:** leading (inspections, observations, toolbox talks, PTW audits) and lagging (incidents by category) indicators per client definitions; overdue actions.
**Hand-offs:** gives PTW to D01/D11, flow-down HSE terms to D05; receives method statements from D01, environmental conditions from D09.
**Escalate to CM when:** any stop-work, incident, regulator visit, or repeated non-compliance by a subcontractor. Stop-work authority is always backed by the CM.

## D09 Environment, Permits & Land
**Mission:** Lawful, released fronts — every permit valid, every condition met, every landowner promise kept and recorded.
**Extract from the contract:** permits held by client vs to be obtained by contractor; permit and planning-approval conditions per section; ecological windows, protected areas, archaeological watch; water abstraction and discharge (hydrotest) conditions (WHG/AwSV as applicable); landowner agreements, entry notices, crop/drainage damage and reinstatement obligations; soil handling (topsoil separation) requirements.
**Standing outputs:** permit register (permit, holder, conditions, validity, responsible dept); condition compliance matrix (condition → action → evidence); front release map (parcel-by-parcel status); landowner contact and commitment log; pre-entry and post-reinstatement condition records (photos, signed); environmental monitoring records.
**Gap triggers:** front needed but parcel not released (G6); permit condition with no owner (G6/G2); permit expiry before activity completion (G6); landowner commitment made on site and not logged (G10); hydrotest water source/discharge not permitted (G6 → D11); condition not flowed down to subcontractor (G4).
**KPIs:** parcels released vs needed per look-ahead; conditions with evidence; landowner complaints open; reinstatement backlog.
**Hand-offs:** gives released fronts to D01/D03, conditions to D08/D05/D11; receives route data from D02.
**Escalate to CM when:** a front blocks the critical path; an authority or landowner threatens stoppage; a condition changes cost or method (→ D04 notice).

## D10 Plant, Equipment & Rentals
**Mission:** Right machine, right place, under a valid PO, and every hire opened and closed with a signed record.
**Extract from the contract:** equipment requirements in the specification (e.g. special lifting, sidebooms, AUT, HDD rig approvals); inspection/certification requirements; emission or noise limits (as given); client requirements for operator competence.
**Standing outputs:** fleet plan vs look-ahead; rental register (supplier, PO, term, rate basis, on-hire date, off-hire date, deposit); on/off-hire inspection records with photos, signed both sides; maintenance and inspection certificates; fuel log; utilisation report.
**Gap triggers:** machine on site without PO (G3 — S-03); hire term expired but still billing or still in use (G3); handover damage not recorded (G10); charges not in PO (first-fuel, transport, cleaning) (G3); certificate expired (G10/HSE); deposit not tracked to refund (G3).
**KPIs:** utilisation; idle-on-hire days; hires without PO; invoices matched to PO and term; deposits outstanding.
**Hand-offs:** receives demand from D01, POs from D05; gives invoice validation data to D12.
**Escalate to CM when:** a critical machine is down without redundancy; a rental dispute over damage or charges exceeds the agreed threshold.

## D11 Testing, Pre-commissioning & Tie-ins
**Mission:** Every test section cleaned, gauged, tested, dewatered and dried to spec, with tie-ins executed inside the shutdown window.
**Extract from the contract:** test sections, test pressure basis and hold times (as given in spec); test medium, water source and disposal; cleaning, gauging/caliper pigging, drying criteria; client/authority witness requirements (e.g. TÜV/expert, as specified); tie-in and shutdown procedure and windows (OGE operations); test documentation and certificates.
**Standing outputs:** test-section plan; test procedures; pigging and test records; water management plan (with D09); tie-in plan with shutdown requests; test readiness checklist (mechanical completion, NDT closed, as-built, permits).
**Gap triggers:** test scope (e.g. caliper pigging/pressure test of OD 50 KSR pipes) not in any subcontract or own-forces plan (G1/G2 — S-02); NDT/NCR open in the test section (G9); water permit missing (G6); shutdown window not confirmed by OGE (G5); test instruments not calibrated (G10).
**KPIs:** sections tested first time; test readiness vs plan; tie-ins inside window.
**Hand-offs:** receives acceptance from D07, permits from D09, as-builts from D02; gives certificates to D13, completion to D12 (milestone).
**Escalate to CM when:** a test fails; a shutdown window is at risk; test scope ownership is disputed.

## D12 Cost Control & Commercial Finance
**Mission:** Know the cost to complete, get paid for what was done, and pay only what was ordered.
**Extract from the contract:** pricing basis (lump sum / remeasured / rates); measurement rules; payment application format, frequency and milestones; retention, advance payment and guarantees; price indexation (if any, as given); invoicing requirements; variation valuation rules; daywork rates.
**Standing outputs:** cost report (budget, committed, actual, forecast, cost to complete) by package; measurement sheets reconciled with D01/D02; payment application and milestone tracker; three-way match (PO ↔ receipt ↔ invoice) exceptions list; cash-flow forecast; variation and claim quantum (with D04).
**Gap triggers:** cost incurred on scope not in BOQ (G1/G3); invoice not matching PO or term (G3); milestone evidence missing at application date (G10); committed cost above budget without approval (G5); DE035/DE042 cost misallocation (G2).
**KPIs:** forecast accuracy; unbilled work; invoice exceptions open; retention and guarantees status; variation value submitted vs recovered.
**Hand-offs:** receives quantities (D01/D02), POs (D05), hire data (D10), completion (D11); gives cost status to CM and D04.
**Escalate to CM when:** forecast exceeds budget or contingency; a milestone payment slips; a supplier dispute affects cash flow.

## D13 Document Control & Handover Dossier
**Mission:** The right revision in everyone's hands, every submission tracked to approval, and a dossier that is complete when the pipe is.
**Extract from the contract:** document numbering and transmittal rules; client review periods and codes; MDR/deliverables list; dossier structure and format (electronic/paper, language); as-built requirements; record retention.
**Standing outputs:** document register and MDR status; transmittal log with client review due dates; superseded-document control on site; dossier index with completeness per section/km; as-built package tracker.
**Gap triggers:** client review overdue against the contract period (G5 → D04 for possible notice); site using superseded revision (quality/G10); dossier records missing for completed sections (G10); deliverable required by contract not in MDR (G1).
**KPIs:** submissions on time; client reviews overdue; dossier completeness vs construction progress.
**Hand-offs:** receives records from all departments; gives approval status to D03 and D04.
**Escalate to CM when:** dossier gaps threaten a milestone or acceptance; client review delays affect the critical path.

## D14 Stakeholder & Client Interface (CM with Project Manager)
**Mission:** One voice to OGE, authorities, the JV and HQ — decisions recorded, nothing promised outside the contract.
**Extract from the contract:** client representative authority and instruction rules; meeting and reporting obligations; communication channels and languages; JV governance (who signs, who approves — DENYS / SICIM).
**Standing outputs:** meeting schedule and minutes with action owners; client instruction log (with D04); monthly report; JV decision log (decision, required approver, approved by, date); stakeholder map (authorities, landowners, utilities, municipalities).
**Gap triggers:** decision taken without the required JV approver (G5); client commitment made in a meeting outside contract (G3 → D04); minutes not issued/agreed (G10).
**KPIs:** actions closed from client meetings; decisions with complete approval trail.
**Escalate to Project Director / JV board when:** entitlement dispute, major cost or schedule change, reputational or authority issue.
