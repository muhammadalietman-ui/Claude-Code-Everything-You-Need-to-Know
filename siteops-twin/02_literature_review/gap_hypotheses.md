# Candidate Research Gaps (to be tested by the SLR)

Owner: literature-reviewer, with research-lead. Version 0.1 (Phase 0).

> Every gap below is a **[A] candidate**, based on the reviewer's prior expectation. It is **not a finding**. Each one is confirmed, partially confirmed or rejected in Phase 2 (`gap_analysis.md`), using the concept matrix (cluster × outcome × context) and the evidence tables.

| ID | Candidate gap | Why it is plausible [A] | How the SLR tests it | Maps to |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| G1 | **Commercial blind spot.** Construction digital-twin research focuses on technical and progress state (geometry, schedule, quality, safety). It rarely addresses commercial control: quantity certification, subcontractor payment and invoice verification. | DT reviews (e.g. S08–S10, VERIFY) are framed around information systems and lifecycle; payment appears mostly in separate blockchain/smart-contract literature | Count C1 papers that are also flagged C6 with a payment or certification outcome. Expect few | Main RQ, RQ4, H3 |
| G2 | **Linear and pipeline under-representation.** Evidence on DT and automated progress monitoring comes mainly from buildings and, to a lesser extent, roads and rail. Cross-country pipeline construction (long ROW, moving work fronts, sequenced crews) is rarely studied. | Pipeline literature concentrates on operations-phase integrity (EX2) | Share of included studies with context = pipeline or linear; check the C1 × C3 intersection | RQ1, RQ2 |
| G3 | **Uncertainty to contract gap.** UAV photogrammetry studies report accuracy (RMSE, volume error) against reference surveys. Few translate that uncertainty into **contractual tolerance or acceptance rules** for certifying quantities. | Accuracy studies end at the metric; commercial studies do not model measurement uncertainty | C2 × C8 papers that also code C6, i.e. those using uncertainty in a decision or acceptance rule | RQ1, H1 |
| G4 | **Missing linear-referenced data model.** There is no established data model that reconciles *planned, reported, measured, claimed, certified and invoiced* quantities against chainage/KP, time, crew, equipment and subcontractor. Standards (IFC 4.3, PODS, LandXML) cover geometry and assets, not commercial quantity states. | Interoperability work targets design-to-asset handover | C7 papers proposing schemas with commercial entities; gap in the standards' scope | RQ2 |
| G5 | **Ethics and practicality of tracking on remote linear sites.** Worker and equipment tracking studies are mostly short trials on compact sites. Evidence on (a) intermittent connectivity over kilometres of ROW and (b) privacy, consent and labour-law constraints for continuous personnel tracking is limited. | Tracking validation is usually done on building sites; ethics is discussed qualitatively | C4 × C4-ETH × context; count studies with field duration of 4 weeks or more | RQ5, H4 |
| G6 | **Weak evidence of commercial outcomes.** Few studies measure the **effect** of digital monitoring on disputes, administrative effort or payment-cycle time with a baseline/intervention design. Most evaluate technical accuracy or user perception only. | Common DSR and prototype evaluation pattern | C6/C5 outcome coding: proportion with a quantitative pre/post or control comparison | H2, H3 |

**[R]** If G1 and G3 are confirmed, they form the core contribution claim: a measurability-aware reconciliation framework that links UAV uncertainty to contractual tolerance. That claim is already anticipated in `05_methodology/research_framing.md` §6.
