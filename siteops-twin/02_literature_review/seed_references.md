# Seed References (not the review)

Owner: literature-reviewer. Version 0.1 (Phase 0).

> **What this list is.** A short **seed** of seminal and key works. It is used for (a) the gold-set test of the search strings and (b) the methodological and standards citations.
>
> **What it is not.** It is **not** the result of the systematic review, and it implies no screening decision.
>
> **Verification.** Every entry has status **VERIFY**. Bibliographic details and DOIs come from the reviewer's prior knowledge and have **not yet been checked** against the publisher record. "Candidate DOI" values must be resolved at doi.org before citation. Blank cells mean unknown. The quality-reviewer blocks any citation still marked VERIFY (R-11).

Citation style: APA 7.
